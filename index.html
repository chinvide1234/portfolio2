About

chinvide

Born 1996, Kumamoto, Japan.

Graduated from the University of Tokyo, Faculty of Engineering.

Through painting, I seek a cosmic perspective from which to question the existence of both self and others.

My practice is an attempt to transcend the limitations of the body.

1996年熊本県生まれ。東京大学工学部卒業。

絵画を通した宇宙的視座、視覚の獲得から自他の存在を問い、肉体からの跳躍を試行する。
