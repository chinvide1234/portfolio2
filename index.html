About

chinvide

Born 1996, Kumamoto, Japan.
Graduated from the University of Tokyo, Faculty of Engineering.

Using motifs of civilization, machines, the body, living organisms, and information,
I reconsider what human beings choose to value in life.

I push the accumulation of my own time, labor, emotions,
and material to their limits within each work.

Through this process,
I seek to assert the value of the self and to exchange existence with the viewer.

文明、機械、身体、生物、情報などをモチーフに、
人間が何を価値として生きるのかを問い直す。

作品には、自分自身の時間、労働、感情、物質を極限まで堆積させる。

自己の価値の証明と、鑑賞者との存在の交換を指向する。
